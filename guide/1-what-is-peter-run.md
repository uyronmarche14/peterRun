# 1 · What is PETER RUN?

## 🎯 In one sentence

PETER RUN is a calm, three-lane street game for Windows where a person practises four leg movements, while a therapist watches and reviews.

## 🌅 The idea

Rehabilitation exercise is repetitive. PETER RUN turns a set of repetitions into a short walk through a Filipino barangay street in the morning. The player is **Peter**. Ahead on the road there are **crates**, a **puddle** and a **laundry line**. Each one asks for one simple movement. The therapist decides how many repetitions to do, and the game keeps count.

## 👥 Who it is for

| Person | What they get |
| --- | --- |
| **Patient** | One clear instruction at a time and no pressure. |
| **Therapist or guardian** | Control of the plan, and a review of what was done. |
| **Device team** | A game that accepts plain keyboard-style input, so a wearable can drive it. |

## 💚 Design principles

> 1. **Calm over fast.** The time to respond is fixed. Difficulty is only the number of repetitions.
> 2. **Clear, not punishing.** No lives, health, score loss or chase. If Peter reaches an obstacle the run ends and everything done is kept.
> 3. **Therapist in control.** Setup, pause and ending are always available and carry no penalty.
> 4. **One thing at a time.** One instruction, each with a picture, a word and a different obstacle shape.
> 5. **Any controller.** Keyboard, gamepad or a wearable all work the same way.
> 6. **Original and local.** Original Filipino setting, art, music and character.

## 🎮 What is in the game today

- A welcome screen with music, **Settings**, a **Tutorial** and **Start Session**.
- **Session setup**: from 1 to 50 repetitions for each of the four movements, and a **game speed** from 1 to 10.
- A **controller check** with a sensitivity choice and a delay test.
- A **3 · 2 · 1 · Go!** start countdown.
- One route: **Barangay Morning**, with a travelling street and living background.
- Many hand-made obstacle arrangements, so runs do not feel the same.
- A **review** at the end with completed and planned repetitions, and a game-speed choice for the next run.
- **Pause**, **End Level** and **End Session** at any time.
- A private **session history** file on the computer (numbers only, no names).

## 🚫 What it is not

- Not a medical device, and not a measure of angles or range of motion.
- Not a competition: no score, ranking or achievements.
- Not a patient-record system: it keeps counts only, and no personal details.
