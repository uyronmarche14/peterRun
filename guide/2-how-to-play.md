# 2 · How to play

> 💡 PETER RUN is a slow, steady street walk. Do **one movement at a time**. The time to respond never gets shorter. There is no score. Take your time.

## 🕐 The idea in one minute

1. Peter stays near the bottom of the screen. The street moves toward him.
2. The road has **three lanes**: left, middle and right.
3. Something appears ahead. The screen shows **one** word, such as **JUMP**.
4. Do that movement. Peter does it too.
5. Watch the result. After a short rest, the next one comes.

## 🦵 The four movements

| Movement | Keyboard | Gamepad | What Peter does |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **MOVE LEFT** | `A` or ← | D-pad left | Steps one lane to the left |
| **MOVE RIGHT** | `D` or → | D-pad right | Steps one lane to the right |
| **JUMP** | `W` or ↑ | Bottom button | Jumps up |
| **SLIDE** | `S` or ↓ | Right button | Ducks and slides low |
| **PAUSE** | `P` | Start / Options | Freezes everything at once |

> ✅ Left always means left and right always means right, whichever leg is being exercised.

## 🚧 What is on the road

Each obstacle looks different so you can tell them apart at a glance.

| Obstacle | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| 📦 **Crates** | **Move** to a lane that is not blocked |
| 💧 **Puddle** | **Jump** over it |
| 👕 **Laundry line** | **Slide** under it |

## 🖥️ Reading the screen

| You see | It means |
| --- | --- |
| **NEXT ACTION** | Nothing is happening yet. Get comfortable. |
| **GET READY** (yellow) | The next movement is shown. Look at it and prepare. |
| **MOVE NOW** (green) | Do the movement now. You have **2 seconds**. |
| **Nice step!** | Right movement. It counts toward your target. |
| **Path is clear.** | You were already in a safe lane. Nothing was needed. |
| **Take your time.** | Not the right movement. Nothing bad happens, and the next one is a little easier. |

A progress display shows how many repetitions you have done out of your target.

## 🚦 Starting a run

A large **3 → 2 → 1 → Go!** appears. While you count, the street moves slowly, Peter walks and the music starts. The controls do not work until **Go!**, so you can settle your feet. At **Go!** the first instruction appears and the street speeds up to its normal pace within half a second.

> ⏱️ The countdown can be 3 seconds, 5 seconds or off. Ask the therapist (it is in **Settings**).

## 💥 If Peter reaches an obstacle

If Peter is in the same lane as an obstacle when it arrives, the run **ends** with the message *"Peter reached an obstacle. Completed repetitions are saved for review."*

- Nothing is lost. Everything you finished is saved.
- There are no lives and no score to lose.
- You can talk with your therapist and try again.

## 🏁 Finishing

- The level is complete when you finish **all the planned repetitions**.
- You can stop early at any time with **End Level** or **End Session**. You are asked to confirm. **There is no penalty.**
- At the end you can choose a **game speed** for the next run, from **1 (faster)** to **10 (slower)**. See [Game speed](6-game-speed.md). You can also finish without choosing.

## 🌱 Tips

- Look at the word first, then the road.
- You do not need to be fast. One careful movement is better than a rushed one.
- If you tire, press **P** to pause and rest. Nothing starts by itself.
- **Practise first**: choose **Tutorial** or **Practice movements**.
