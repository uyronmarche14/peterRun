# How to Play

PETER RUN is a slow, steady street walk. You do **one movement at a time** to guide Peter past what is on the road. The response time never speeds up and there is no score. Take your time.

## The idea in one minute

1. Peter stays near the bottom of the screen. The street moves toward him.
2. The road has **three lanes**: left, middle and right.
3. Something appears ahead on the road. The screen shows **one** word, such as **JUMP**.
4. Do that movement. Peter does it too.
5. Watch the result, rest a moment, and the next one comes.

## The four movements

| Movement | Keyboard | What Peter does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **MOVE LEFT** | `A` | Steps one lane to the left |
| **MOVE RIGHT** | `D` | Steps one lane to the right |
| **JUMP** | `W` | Jumps up |
| **SLIDE** | `S` | Ducks and slides low |
| **PAUSE** | `P` | Freezes everything immediately |

Left always means left and right always means right, whichever leg is being exercised.

## What is on the road

Each kind of obstacle looks different so you can tell them apart at a glance.

| Obstacle | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Crates** | **Move** to a lane that is not blocked (left or right, as shown) |
| **Puddle** | **Jump** over it |
| **Laundry line** | **Slide** under it |

## Reading the screen

| You see | It means |
| --- | --- |
| **NEXT ACTION** | Nothing is happening yet. Get comfortable. |
| **GET READY** (yellow) | The next movement is shown. Look at it and prepare. You have about 2½ seconds at standard speed; less at game speed 1 and more at speed 10. |
| **MOVE NOW** (green) | Do the movement now. You have about 2 seconds. |
| **Nice step!** | You did the right movement. It counts toward your target. |
| **Path is clear.** | You were already in a safe lane. Nothing needed doing. |
| **Take your time.** | The movement was not the right one. Nothing bad happens, and the next one will be a little easier. |

A progress display shows how many of each movement you have done out of your target.

## If Peter reaches an obstacle

If Peter ends up in the same lane as an obstacle, the run **ends** with the message *"Peter reached an obstacle. Completed repetitions are saved for review."*

- Nothing is lost. Everything you completed is saved for review.
- There are no lives and no score to lose.
- You can talk with your therapist and try again.

## Starting a run

When a run begins, a large **3 → 2 → 1 → Go!** countdown appears (a 5-second countdown or no countdown can be chosen in Settings). The street moves slowly and Peter walks while you count, and the music starts at the first number. The controls do nothing until **Go!**. Use the countdown to settle your feet and look at the screen. At **Go!** the first instruction appears and the street eases up to full speed over about half a second.

## Finishing

- The level is complete when you have done **all the planned repetitions**. A message says so.
- You can also stop early at any time with **End Level** or **End Session**. You are asked to confirm, and **there is no penalty for ending early.**
- At the end you choose the game speed for the next run, from **1 (faster) to 10 (slower)**. It changes the road, Peter's stride and how long GET READY lasts, never the 2 seconds you have to move. It is fine to finish without choosing.

## Tips

- Look at the word on screen first, then the road.
- You do not need to be fast. One careful movement is better than a rushed one.
- If you tire, press **P** to pause and rest. Nothing starts by itself.
- You can **practice** before a session: choose **Tutorial** or **Practice movements**.
